YOUR DATA

Private by account

By default, review text is analyzed in this browser. If you click Improve ranking with AI, up to 12 review excerpts and up to 18 current film summaries are sent to your selected AI provider for ranking. That provider processes the request under its own privacy terms. The local AI cache stores movie IDs, scores, reasons, and model metadata, not review text. Your imported library follows FrameRate’s existing sync settings.
